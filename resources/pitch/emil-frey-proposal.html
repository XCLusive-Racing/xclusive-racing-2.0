01 · Who We Are

We do not just play racing games.
We build careers, communities, and champions.

XCLusive Racing was born from the Xbox Community League, the most dominant force in ACC console racing, which was founded in 2023. It has grown into one of Europe's largest sim racing organisations and has now expanded from console to PC across every major platform. Competitive, rated championships on ACC, iRacing, and Le Mans Ultimate. Our own XCL Rating system across seven licence tiers. An esports arm. And real-world connections through drivers competing in professional motorsport right now.

The Community

8,000+ Racers.
Not Followers.

8,000+ registered members who race in rated championships, track their XCL Rating, and invest in real sim hardware. Not a social following. A self-selected pool of high-intent racers. All of that came from console alone, in just three years. With XCLusive now expanding across multiple platforms, that number has real potential to grow fast.

Our own Elo-based XCL Rating across seven tiers, from Rookie to Legend. Objective talent identification. No politics, just performance. We have scouts with a proven track record, ready to bring in new talent, and experienced team members helping across every aspect of racing and event management.

  • The global sim racing market is projected to exceed 10 billion dollars by 2030, with esports audiences rivalling traditional motorsport broadcasts.

08 · The Team Proposal

Not another
esports team.
The next legend.

Born from the most dominant force in ACC console racing, the Xbox Community League, XCLusive Racing is now expanding to PC across all three major sim platforms. The community, the drivers, and the infrastructure are in place. The missing piece is a name that matters. Emil Frey.


Emil Frey gets naming rights, stream overlay branding across 36+ esports drivers who become instant Emil Frey ambassadors, a logo on real-world championship cars in Porsche Supercup, rally, and MX5 Cup, and a direct line to a large and fast-growing community that will constantly see, know, and aspire to Emil Frey. What drivers get in return is not cash. It is access. Events. Real-world connections. A brand that matters in motorsport, and a genuine ladder into the Emil Frey world.

Option A

Full Rebrand

The esports team races as Emil Frey Esports across every platform and every real-world car we field. One identity, top to bottom.

Option B

Branding Overlay

XCLusive keeps its name and Emil Frey branding is applied across the platform, the team, the socials, and every touchpoint.

What Emil Frey Gets
  • An esports team competing across ACC, iRacing, LMU, and beyond.
  • Brand presence across the platform, the socials, and real-world championship cars.
  • A pipeline of young talent and brand affinity among thousands of members.
  • A grassroots story no closed elite squad can offer.
  • A real entry into esports beyond the single Verstappen.com cooperation.
What We Ask In Return
  • One official connection between XCLusive and Emil Frey.
  • The ability to offer our members a real dream: a path from their first online race to a job in sim racing or motorsport.
