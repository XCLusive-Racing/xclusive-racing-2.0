01 · Who We Are

You have not met us.
Let us fix that.

XCLusive Racing started as the Xbox Community League, the most dominant force in ACC console racing. It has grown into one of Europe's largest sim racing organisations. 11,000+ registered members. Competitive, rated championships on ACC, iRacing, and Le Mans Ultimate. Our own XCL Rating system across seven licence tiers. An esports arm. And real-world connections through drivers competing right now in professional motorsport.

The Community

11,000+ Racers.
Not Followers.

11,000+ registered members who race in rated championships, track their XCL Rating, and invest in real sim hardware. Not a social following. A self-selected pool of high-intent racers.

The System

Every Member,
Measured.

Our own Elo-based XCL Rating across seven tiers, from Rookie to Legend. Every member is measured. Talent surfaces on its own.

The Real-World Link

Already Racing,

02 · The Vision

The dream we are selling.
One home for racing.

A single platform. News, events, a sim racing team, and a real-world team, all in one place. A young racer joins for the racing and stays for the future. That is the product. Emil Frey Racing is the name that makes the dream real.

Picture a thirteen year old on a console. They watch Emil Frey Racing win at the top of GT racing. It looks impossibly far away. There is no door.

XCLusive is the door.

We run events across every major sim, Assetto Corsa, Assetto Corsa Competizione, Le Mans Ultimate, iRacing, and RaceRoom, on PC and on console. Most matchmaking platforms serve PC only, and often a single game. XCLusive brings them together in one smart system.

Inside that system, a beginner can climb. They can help build the platform, or get fast enough to land on our radar. Our scouting programme, time trials, and XCL Rating pre-select the talent worth backing. When the esports team they are chasing wears the Emil Frey name, there is no reason to look anywhere else.

03 · Why Emil Frey

Not just a race team.
A destination.

Team Redline gives a partner the top of the pyramid. A closed, elite squad. XCLusive gives you the whole pyramid, and the pipeline that feeds it. That matters more for Emil Frey than for anyone, because Emil Frey is not only a race team. It is Europe's largest automotive group. 22,000+ people across twelve countries. Dealerships, marketing, engineering, and a works GT programme.

Our members are thousands of young, motorsport obsessed people looking for a way in. You are the company with the doors. Social media, engineering, coaching, sim training for real drivers, and for the quick ones, a real seat. The talent flows up through XCLusive and into the Emil Frey world.

The Emil Frey name attracts top drivers. Top drivers win. Winning attracts the next thousand members. The loop builds itself.

06 · The Proposal

Two paths.
Your call.

We keep running the team as we do today, competing at the highest level, at no operational cost to you. Involvement scales entirely to how visible Emil Frey wants to be. The team identity is where we offer two options.

Option A

Full Rebrand

The esports team races as Emil Frey Racing Esports across every platform and every real-world car we field. One identity, top to bottom.

Option B

Branding Overlay

XCLusive keeps its name and Emil Frey branding is applied across the platform, the team, the socials, and every touchpoint. Your name everywhere the community lives.

What Emil Frey Gets
  • An esports team competing across ACC, iRacing, LMU, and beyond.
  • Brand presence across the platform, the socials, and real-world championship cars.
  • A pipeline of young talent and brand affinity among thousands of members.
  • A grassroots story no closed elite squad can offer.
  • A real entry into esports beyond the single Verstappen.com cooperation.
What We Ask In Return
  • One official connection between XCLusive and Emil Frey.
  • The ability to offer our members a real dream: a path from their first online race to a job in sim racing or motorsport.
