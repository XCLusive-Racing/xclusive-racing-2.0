01 — The Opportunity

Sim racing
grew up.
This is what's next.

The Le Mans Virtual proved the format. The FIA F4 Esports Championship — sponsored by MOZA, run on iRacing, with a $35,000 prize pool — proved the sanctioning model. No one has combined them at scale: a full virtual endurance calendar, running simultaneously with the real events, Division 1 on-site at the circuit. That is the gap. That is this proposal.

01

The Real Race Precedent

The 24H Nürburgring, 24H Spa, 24H Daytona, 24H Le Mans, and 12H Bathurst collectively attract millions of circuit visitors and hundreds of millions of global broadcast viewers every year. A virtual championship running in parallel captures the same audience, the same weekend, at a fraction of the production cost.

02

The Gap in the Market

No FIA-sanctioned virtual championship currently runs simultaneously with real endurance events and requires Division 1 teams to race on-site at the actual circuit. That combination — sim race on circuit during the real race — is new. The dedicated circuit pavilion, the screens, the crowd watching sim and real racing side by side. That is the activation no one has built yet.

The absolute top of sim racing — Williams Esports, Team Redline, Ferrari Esports, Alpine, R8G — are competing at the highest level but facing a real problem: there are not enough elite events, not enough serious competition, and not enough prize pool to match their ambition. The Virtual 24H calendar solves that. It gives the best teams in the world the events they are missing — and gives their sponsors the global audience they are looking for.

03

XCLusive Racing's Position

8,000+ registered members. 37 professional and esports drivers. Active championships on ACC, iRacing and LMU. A broadcast partner — TRTN — already in place. The community, the infrastructure, and the driver relationships exist. The missing piece is a title partner with the hardware capability and brand ambition to make Division 1 on-site possible.

04

Build4Performance's Role

Division 1 requires high-end simulators on-site at the circuit. That is Build4Performance's core product — and this event is the highest-stakes, most visible deployment of that product imaginable. These are the same simulators used by professionals at events like Le Mans Virtual. The pros use your products. There is no better sales pitch than that. Every camera angle that shows the simulator bays at circuit is a live product demonstration to the most qualified audience in motorsport.

02 — The Virtual 24H Events

The 24H races
the world
watches.

Five of the greatest endurance races in motorsport. One virtual championship running alongside each of them. Same circuit. Same race distance. Same weekend. Division 1 teams race on-site. The world watches both.

Each event in the calendar is anchored to its real-world counterpart. Qualifying rounds in the weeks before determine which teams race on-site (Division 1), which race online simultaneously (Division 2), and which compete in the development series (Division 3 Rookie).

Platform partner and circuit agreements are to be confirmed with Build4Performance. XCLusive Racing is open to the platform that best serves the partnership — AC, ACC, iRacing, Le Mans Ultimate — or another platform to be agreed together with Build4Performance.

07 — Roadmap

From agreement
to race day.

Six months from partnership confirmation to the first on-site event. The team rebrand happens in weeks. The championship builds audience through the qualifier series. The first on-site 24H final is the Season 1 flagship moment.

Month 1

Agreement & Rebrand

Partnership signed. Team renamed. Website updated. Stream overlays rebranded. FIA sanctioning application submitted.

Month 2

Public Announcement

Press release via TRTN. Social campaign. Community announcement. Platform partner confirmed. Elite team invitations issued.

Month 3

Qualifier Series Opens

First online qualifier rounds. Division placings begin to take shape. Community Rookie division opens. Broadcast begins.

Month 4–5

Qualifier Finals

Division 1 grid confirmed. On-site logistics activated with B4P. Circuit agreements finalised. Division 2 and 3 grids locked.

Month 5

Pre-Event Activation

B4P simulators delivered and set up at circuit venue. Media coverage begins. Sponsor activations confirmed. Broadcast schedule published.

Month 6

First 24H On-Site Event

Division 1 races at the circuit. Division 2 & 3 online simultaneously. TRTN live broadcast. FIA ceremony. Season 2 announced.
